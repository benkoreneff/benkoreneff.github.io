Workset Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Workset is a gym tracker that runs entirely on your iPhone. It has no account, no server, and no analytics. The developer cannot see your training data, because it never leaves your device unless you send it somewhere yourself.

What is stored

Workouts, exercises, sets, notes, body weight and your app settings are stored in a database inside the app on your device. Nothing is uploaded, because the app makes no network connections at all.

What is not collected

Workset collects no usage analytics, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers and no location. It does not read health or fitness data from other apps, and it contains no third-party code that could collect any of it. It does not track you across apps or websites.

When data leaves the device

Only when you send it. Export Data writes a CSV file and hands it to the iOS share sheet — where it goes from there is your choice. Import Data reads a file you pick. Neither happens on its own.

Permissions

Workset asks for one permission: notifications, used only to tell you the rest timer has finished while you are in another app. Notifications are local to the device, and the timer works whether or not you allow them. The Lock Screen and Dynamic Island Live Activity is rendered on device by iOS.

Children

Workset collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. There is no copy anywhere else, so export first if you want to keep your history.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this page is updated in the same release.

Contact
